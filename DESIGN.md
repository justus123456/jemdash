# Design

Maintained with frontend-god-mode. Read before changing the UI.

## Aesthetic direction
Energetic automotive editorial: orange hero, a tilted photographic print, oversized friendly typography and quiet cream sections.

## Dials
Design variance 8/10; motion intensity 3/10; visual density 4/10.

## Type stack
Display: Outfit, 500–800. Body: DM Sans, 400–700. Google Fonts with swap and sans-serif fallback. Body line-height 1.65; maximum reading width 65ch.

## Color tokens
Orange #f65b30; ink #242620; cream #f8f6ef; muted #62635a; border #d7d6ca; supporting sage #e5e9ce. Warm shadow #822c2030.

## Motion
250ms cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) transform transitions. No autoplay. Reduced-motion disables smooth scrolling and transitions.

## Layout
1440px maximum; 5% desktop and 6% mobile gutters; 90px desktop and 55px mobile section spacing. Asymmetric 48/52 hero; editorial paired sections. Single column below 760px.

## Component inventory
Mobile navigation, enquiry anchors, image fallback, selectable two-photo gallery, specifications, four rental steps, benefit list, native FAQ disclosures, contact block, footer. Lucide SVG icons.

## Brand voice and project-specific bans
Warm, direct, practical. No invented specifications, rates, reviews, guarantees, policies or statistics. Reference photos must be labelled. No accounts, payments or booking confirmation.

## Accessibility floor
Semantic landmarks, skip link, visible focus, native controls, 44px touch targets, alt text, reserved image space and reduced-motion support.
